Dogs in the Field


The culmination of our efforts in breeding occurs in the field. The correct coat for warmth. A correct head for picking up a bird. Correct structure for efficient movement in the water or on land. An intense desire to retrieve through inclement conditions. The capacity to sit patiently on a day when game is sparse. Healthy dogs who enjoy their job well into their senior years. We are most proud seeing our dogs doing what they were bred for. Please feel free to send photos of your Mercer dog on a hunt!
