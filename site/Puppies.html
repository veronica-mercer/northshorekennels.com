Reserving a Puppy

Puppy availability is determined by order of receipt of deposit. We usually breed 1-2 litters per year. It is not uncommon for a litter to be fully reserved before they are born.

Puppy selections take place between 8 and 9.5 weeks of age. We do not breed a litter simply to sell puppies. Each litter is produced in hopes that one or two will be strong additions to the genepool. of one or two puppies in a litter until they reach approximately 9 weeks of age. All puppies are sold on a non-breeding contract unless arrangements have been made prior to submitting a deposit. Reservations are accepted for gender but not colour. Our litters average 6-10 puppies and gender splits vary widely. In cases where a puppy is not available, deposits are 100% refundable (or the deposit can be transferred to a later litter at the buyers option). Any other refunds are at our discretion. Puppy selections are made by us based on your input, household situation, activities you intend to partake in with your new partner and our years of experience.

We do not sell puppies with solely online contact. A telephone conversation gives us both the opportunity to learn a little more and ensure that one of our litters is the right match for your family. Visits are welcome. We look forward to speaking with you!

Please call 519-689-7408. 5-8pm weeknights or 10am-8pm weekends are the best times to reach us.
